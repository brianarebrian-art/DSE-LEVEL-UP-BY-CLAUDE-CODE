# Code of Conduct

DSE Level Up is a free study space for Hong Kong secondary students (roughly ages 12–18), families, and volunteer contributors. We pledge to keep participation harassment-free.

## Our standards

**Expected**

- Be respectful; assume good faith.
- Use clear language; Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) or English is welcome.
- Protect student privacy: do not share other people’s personal data.
- Report security issues privately (see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)).

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, hate speech, sexual content involving minors, or doxxing.
- Attempts to steal student data, bypass login, or attack the live site.
- Submitting copyrighted HKEAA exam materials as if they were ours.
- Publicly posting exploit details that put students at risk.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **dselevelup@gmail.com** (same channel as `security.txt`).

Maintainers may warn, reject contributions, or remove access for violations. This is a small volunteer-run project; we do not claim a paid moderation team.
