## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in DSE LEVEL UP a harassment-free experience for everyone, including students aged roughly 12–18, contributors, and maintainers.

## Standards

- Be respectful; assume good faith.
- No harassment, hate speech, sexual content involving minors, or doxxing.
- No attempts to exfiltrate student data, bypass auth, or attack production.
- Do not submit copyrighted HKEAA exam materials as if they were ours.

## Enforcement

Report to: **dselevelup@gmail.com** (same channel as `security.txt`).  
Maintainers may warn, reject contributions, or ban for violations.  
This draft does **not** invent a legal entity or paid moderation team.
