# EdTech vision — pragmatic, not hype

## What AI can help with (here)

- Personalised practice selection (weak topics, unseen-first).
- Instant MC explanations already in the bank.
- Mistake diagnosis via reverse-log causes (student-chosen).
- Suggested paths from topic stats — always framed as practice hints, not destiny.
- Teacher/volunteer load reduction via AI **draft** triage (`question_revisions`) that still needs humans.
- Risk: fluent wrong explanations; over-trust; content drift from syllabus.

## Hard boundaries (emotion & safety)

- AI must **not** diagnose mental illness.
- AI must **not** replace counsellors or teachers.
- On crisis language: show human help resources; do not role-play therapy.
- No personality judgement (“你係唔努力”).
- No public leaderboards or shame streaks (charter).
- Crisis-adjacent flows stay human-visible (founders / support email).

## Emotional safety learning mode (product)

Already / to reinforce:

- Gentle copy; optional comfort / a11y panel.
- Hide timer; no punishment lock on honest written self-assessment.
- Progress private; sync opt-in after consent.
- Rest-day picker; “enough today” exits.
- Help / relax entry points with HK resources.

## Honesty rule

If we cannot verify a claim (official cut-scores, guaranteed grade lift), we do not show it as fact. Prefer `practiceBand` language over fake DSE certificates from ten questions.
