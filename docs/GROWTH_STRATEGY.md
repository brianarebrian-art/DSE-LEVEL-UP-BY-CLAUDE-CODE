# Growth strategy — DSE Level Up

Positioning statement:

> **DSE Level Up is safe, transparent, SEN-friendly learning infrastructure for the HKDSE — permanently free, bilingual, and honest about what is (and is not) officially endorsed.**

Do not lead with “free” alone. Lead with trust + usefulness.

## Credibility system

1. **Review badges (content, not vanity)**  
   - `machine_checked` — parametric / gate scripts  
   - `human_reviewed` — quality desk decision approve  
   - `community_draft` — not yet human-approved  
   Show status + last updated date on subject / question surfaces when data exists (`dse_question_quality_reviews`, bank versions).
2. **Public quality policy** — link charter + this workflow; publish weekly hotspot summaries (aggregates only).
3. **Error → fix log** — admin marks `fixed`; optional changelog snippet for high-traffic questions.
4. **Invite reviewers** — teachers, uni students, SEN supporters via `REVIEWER_EMAILS` roles; never require student PII to volunteer.

## Word of mouth

- Shareable practice band card (session language, not fake official levels) — already aligned with `practiceBand`.
- Mistake book + rest-day + exam countdown as “plan” artefacts.
- One-concept shorts for social (see platforms).
- Partner blueprint: school social workers, SEN NGOs, tutor communities — pitch emotional safety + free forever, not lead gen.

## Competitive wedge

Not “another MC bank”. The wedge is: **emotional safety + SEN affordances + transparent review + sync without selling data + original rewritten items**. Competitors that scrape papers or push panic timers lose on trust.
