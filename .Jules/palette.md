## 2026-11-03 - Practice Flow Bookmark Touch & Focus Consistency
**Learning:** In practice flow components (`app/practice/*`, `components/BookmarkButton.tsx`), all interactive controls are strictly bound by a 48px touch-target rule (`min-h-12`) checked via `lib/__tests__/practice-touch-targets.test.mts` which prohibits `min-h-11`. Interactive popup drawers within these controls (like the folder selector in BookmarkButton) must simultaneously maintain the `min-h-12` standard while ensuring `focus-visible:outline focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-accent` rings are applied to both chip buttons and inputs for keyboard focus visibility.
**Action:** When enhancing nested interactive dialogs/popovers in practice flow components, check `practice-touch-targets.test.mts` and apply `focus-visible` styling with `outline-accent` alongside `min-h-12` touch heights.
